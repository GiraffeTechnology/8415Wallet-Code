# Future-effective-time risk wording

PRD section 4.6 requires factual measurements without inventing finality or policy.

The prior risk copy claimed any far-future effective time permanently ended the projection because no later entry could exceed it. Strictly increasing uint64 effective times do not imply that: a larger representable effective time can exist. The terminal representability limit is the uint64 maximum, not the UI's one-year observation threshold.

The rule carried everywhere in this repository is: an admitted effective time ahead of now blocks every later entry whose effective time is not greater than it; that bounds admission, and does not by itself permanently end the projection. A later entry may still be admitted, but only once one is actually registered, and that requires all three of a strictly greater effective time, genuine registration evidence, and the time rules the applicable profile imposes — among them its limit on how far ahead an effective time may be. `contracts/reference/RegisterProjectionReference.sol` caps that distance at `MAX_EFFECTIVE_AHEAD` and reverts `EffectiveAtTooFar` beyond it, so under the reference profile the block lifts as `block.timestamp` advances; the reference contract carries no state transition that ends a projection. Only the `uint64` maximum is terminal.

The risk surface now distinguishes future time from elapsed standing time and states the representability limit without treating a future value alone as proof of an unrecoverable state. Existing threshold, presence flags, SDK reads and authority/transaction behavior are unchanged. No finality is recomputed. The profile bound in `tests/invariants.test.ts` is retained: a profile that refuses an effectiveAt too far ahead of the clock is correct, and that boundary case is not a restatement of the withdrawn wording.

Two display regression cases are authored with a hypothetical memory-reader override; they are NOT_RUN and are not admissible-entry, EVM or public-chain evidence. Assigned Linux validation owner should run node --test tests/riskSurfaces-future.test.ts and the existing complete pipeline.
